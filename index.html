How this works

  • Profile — 50 quick taps on how you like to travel. Done once, kept on this phone.
  • Trip — 20 questions for this specific trip: where, dates, people, car, stay preference.
  • Plan — live weather, real driving distance and fuel cost for your Innova or Baleno, then Claude searches ixigo, RailOne, MakeMyTrip, Airbnb, TripAdvisor, Reddit and news for the destination and writes a day-by-day itinerary with every expense in ₹.
  • Go — during the trip: checklist per day, log what you actually spent, compare to plan.
